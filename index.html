Privacy Policy for Nitxy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

Nitxy collects the following information:

  • Audio Recordings: We request microphone permission to allow voice-based features for educational purposes and basically it is used for language translation purpose only. This may include voice notes, audio questions, or future speech-based learning features.
  • App Usage Data: We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience and identify popular features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing voice-based educational features in our application
  • Improving app functionality and user experience
  • Personalizing learning content for CBSE students (Classes 10-12)
  • Analyzing app performance and fixing issues

3. Data Storage & Security

Audio Data: All audio recordings are processed locally on your device and are not stored on our servers and these are used for translation purpose only.

Usage Data: Anonymous usage statistics are stored securely and used only for analytical purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not share your personal data with third parties. The App does not use third-party analytics or advertising services that collect personal information.

5. Data Retention

We retain audio data only as long as necessary to provide the educational features. You can delete any saved audio recordings at any time through the App.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of data collection

7. Children's Privacy

Nitxy is designed for CBSE students in Classes 10-12. We are committed to protecting the privacy of young users and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
