Privacy Policy for Nitxy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

Nitxy collects the following information:

  • Audio Recordings: We request microphone permission to allow voice-based features for educational purposes. This may include voice notes, audio questions, or future speech-based learning features.
  • App Usage Data: We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience and identify popular features.
  • Account Information: If you create an account, we store your email address and basic profile information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing voice-based educational features
  • Improving app functionality and user experience
  • Personalizing learning content for CBSE students (Classes 10-12)
  • Analyzing app performance and fixing issues
  • Managing user accounts and preferences

3. Data Storage & Security

Audio Data: All audio recordings are processed locally on your device and are not stored on our servers unless you explicitly choose to save them for future reference.

Usage Data: Anonymous usage statistics are stored securely and used only for analytical purposes.

Account Data: User account information is stored securely on our servers with encryption.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not share your personal data with third parties. The App does not use third-party analytics or advertising services that collect personal information.

5. Data Retention

We retain audio data only as long as necessary to provide the educational features. You can delete any saved audio recordings at any time through the App. Account data is retained until you request deletion.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of data collection
  • Export your data

7. Children's Privacy

Nitxy is designed for CBSE students in Classes 10-12. We are committed to protecting the privacy of young users and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Data Deletion Requests

If you wish to delete your account and associated data from Nitxy, please follow these steps:

  1. Open the Nitxy app and go to Profile page and you can find Delete Account
  2. Tap on "Delete Account"
  3. Confirm your request

Alternatively, you can contact us directly at nitxy07@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your registered email address.

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm once your data has been completely deleted from our systems.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or data deletion, please contact us at:

Email: nitxy07@gmail.com

Response Time: We typically respond within 2-3 business days.
